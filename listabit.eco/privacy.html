Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Chrome extension

Listabit reads the URL of the tab you are currently viewing, only to check whether the same product is in our database. This check happens locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or share your browsing history. We do not track which websites you visit, and we do not send your browsing history to our servers.

When a match is found, the extension shows the other links from our database. No personal data is shown in the extension, only the product link, city, county, country, condition, and size if provided.

Link submissions

If you submit links through the form on listabit.eco, we store the links, the optional city, and the optional note you provide, so we can review them manually. We do not ask for your name or email address in the form.
