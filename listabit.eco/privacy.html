Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Listabit is a Chrome extension and website that helps you find the same product in other shops, including used products and products for parts, near you.

Contact: listabitapp@gmail.com

What the extension does

When you are viewing a product page and you click the Listabit icon, the extension shows you the same product listed in other shops, with the shop domain, city and condition. The extension also shows a badge on the icon when a matching product is found, so you can see at a glance whether other listings exist.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information. We do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising identifiers. We do not read the content of the pages you visit, we do not track which websites you visit beyond the single URL needed for the lookup, and we do not store your browsing history. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Third parties

Supabase, Inc. is our database and hosting provider. The current tab URL is sent to Supabase solely to look up the same product. Data is not sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers. See the Supabase privacy policy for how Supabase handles server logs.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Data at rest is stored using strong encryption provided by Supabase.

Link submissions

If you submit links through the form on listabit.eco, we store the links, city, country, condition, size and note you provide, so we can review them manually. We do not ask for your name or email address in the form. Submitted links are public product links, and we do not remove them on request.

Your rights

If you have a question about your data, or you want us to delete any data we hold about you, contact us at listabitapp@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this policy and disclose the change prominently in the extension and on listabit.eco before the change takes effect.
