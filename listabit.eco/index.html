How it works

1. Browse as usual. Open a product page in Chrome.
2. See the badge. The Listabit icon shows a number when the same product is in our database.
3. Click and choose. See the other links, with city, county, country and condition.

What Listabit finds

Used products
Products for parts
Products for rent
Products near you, in your own city

Listabit is not a price comparison site. The goal is reuse and sustainability.
