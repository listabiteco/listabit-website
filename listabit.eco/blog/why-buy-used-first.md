# Why buy used first?

The best product is often the one that already exists.

When you buy used, you save resources, you save money, and you often get the product faster, from someone near you.

## What to check first

- Does it work, or is it only good for parts?
- Is it in your city, so you can see it before you buy?
- Can you rent it instead, if you only need it once?

## How Listabit helps

Listabit shows you the same product in other shops, used, for parts, or for rent, near you. Open a product page, see the badge, and choose the reused option when it fits.

Buy new only when used does not fit. That is the whole idea.
